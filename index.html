This is a classic crash game. The plane takes off and the multiplier rises from 1.00×.

Cash out before the plane flies away and you win your stake × the current multiplier. If you wait too long, the stake is lost.

The crash point is determined at the start of every round and is never shown until the plane disappears. There is no skill timing against a visible zone — only when you decide to take the money.

Growth: mult(t) = 1 + (t ÷ 8.0)1.35

Demo mode uses practice pts. Real balance is in BIRR (starts at 0) and only changes from bets.
